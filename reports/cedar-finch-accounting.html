Method

Each control is scored 0-3 for maturity and mapped to NIST CSF 2.0. Likelihood (1-5) comes from maturity; impact (1-5) is set per control and raised by 1 for data-related controls when sensitive data is held. Risk = likelihood × impact (Low 1-5, Medium 6-11, High 12-19, Critical 20-25). The risk index scales total risk from 0 (every control strong) to 100 (every control missing). Actions are ranked by risk reduction ÷ effort.
